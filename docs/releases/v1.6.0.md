UsageBeacon v1.6.0 warns you before you hit a limit, shows reset countdowns without opening the popup, and makes the popup easier to use from the keyboard and with a screen reader.

![Widget tooltip with reset countdowns](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/kmch4n/UsageBeacon/v1.6.0/docs/images/widget-tooltip.png)

## Highlights

- **Usage alerts.** A Windows notification appears the first time a five-hour or weekly limit reaches 80%, and again at 95%, for Claude, Codex, and (when shown) Gemini. Each limit window alerts at most once per threshold, and restarting the app inside a window does not repeat them. Alerts are on by default; turn them off with "Notify at 80% and 95% usage" in the popup.
- **Reset countdowns in the widget tooltip.** Hover the widget to see every period's percentage and the time left until it resets, whether or not the weekly widget layout is enabled.
- **Hide the widget for screen sharing.** The tray menu can hide the widget for one hour or until restart. Usage keeps updating, and "Show widget" brings it back.
- **Popup accessibility.** Settings are announced with their labels, the refresh and close buttons have spoken names, keyboard focus shows a themed ring, Tab cycles within the popup, and Escape closes it.

## Maintenance

- CI builds now fail on compiler warnings.

Download the self-contained `UsageBeacon.exe` below and compare its SHA-256 hash with `UsageBeacon.exe.sha256`. The executable is unsigned, so Windows SmartScreen may show a warning.
