UsageBeacon v1.6.2 shows the separate Gemini 4 Argon quota that recent Antigravity CLI releases report, so Gemini no longer reads 0% while you use Argon.

![Popup with the Gemini 4 Argon weekly row](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/kmch4n/UsageBeacon/v1.6.2/docs/images/popup.png)

## Fixed

- **Gemini 4 Argon usage is shown.** agy 1.4 reports Gemini 4 Argon as its own weekly quota, separate from the shared Gemini Flash/Pro limits. UsageBeacon read only the shared limits, so with Argon as your model Gemini stayed at 0%. Now:
  - The popup's Antigravity section adds a **Weekly (Argon)** row with its own bar and reset time.
  - The widget's Gemini weekly value shows whichever of the shared and Argon weekly limits is fuller. The five-hour value still comes from the shared limit, because Argon has no five-hour limit.
  - The widget tooltip lists the Argon weekly percentage and its reset countdown.
  - Usage alerts at 80% and 95% also cover the Argon weekly limit.

Reports without an Argon group look the same as before.

Download the self-contained `UsageBeacon.exe` below and compare its SHA-256 hash with `UsageBeacon.exe.sha256`. The executable is unsigned, so Windows SmartScreen may show a warning.
